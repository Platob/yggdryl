Move the market vocabulary into yggdryl-market and FIX into yggdryl-fix

The market enum kinds (side, timeinforce, marketdatakind, marketdatatype),
the graph's market leaves and their walk, the identifier vocabulary, the
ISIN registry, the limit and EUSIPA values, the market message and the
anomaly now live in rust/market as the yggdryl-market crate, and the FIX
dictionary, codec, message handle and lifecycle in rust/fix as yggdryl-fix.
Each claims what it brings through install(): the four kinds on the market
register at the bytes, names and ranks the core held them at, so every hash
and order pin is byte-identical, and the FIX Latest logical names on the
logical-name register. The core keeps the extension points and the
implementer module the two crates reach it through; a market name no claim
answers is refused naming the crate to install. The core's tests that used a
kind only as a fixture are re-fixtured on the core's own types and stay; the
tests that pin a market or FIX fact move with their crate. The bindings and
the CLI depend on both crates and install them at init; the CI planner runs
each leaf's own jobs. No alias, no shim: every caller spells the crate it
links.
