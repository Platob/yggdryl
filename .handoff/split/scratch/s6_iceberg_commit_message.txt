Move the Iceberg table format into yggdryl-iceberg

`yggdryl-iceberg` at `rust/iceberg/` holds the Iceberg tables - metadata,
manifests, scans, commits, the `hadoop` catalog - and, under its `s3tables`
feature, Amazon S3 Tables, linking `yggdryl-avro`, `yggdryl-parquet` and
`yggdryl-s3`. `install()` claims `ICEBERG_FORMAT`, `HADOOP_FACTORY` and the
table bucket's factory and locator on the core's registers, once; the
bindings' init and the `yggdryl` command call it. `IcebergField` is minted in
the crate over `Scheme::ICEBERG`, and the core keeps the type vocabulary
(`yggdryl::iceberg::PrimitiveType`), loses its `iceberg` and `s3tables`
features and its seeds, and reaches the crate's needs through
`yggdryl::implementer`.
