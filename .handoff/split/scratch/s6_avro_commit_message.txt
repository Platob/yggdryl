Move the Avro medium into yggdryl-avro

Avro is the crate `yggdryl-avro` at `rust/avro/`: the schemas, datums,
object containers, schema resolution, single-object framing and the
record medium, with its tests, the Avro rows of the shared record
benchmarks and the fastavro exchange beside it. `install()` claims
`AVRO_CODEC` on the core's register once per process at rank 2, which
`media::RESERVED_RANKS` keeps for it, so every options value hashes and
orders as it did; the bindings' init and the CLI's `main` call it. The
crate's hidden `implementer` carries the container, cursor and datum
codec Iceberg's manifests read.

The core no longer holds `avro`, its seed claim or `snap`. `claim`
admits a reserved rank under its own name alone. `yggdryl::implementer`
gains the record doors, dimensions, schema, cache clock, cast and value
helpers a media crate reaches, and `PlanCache` moves into it. The core
tests that build Avro objects move byte-identical to the crate's tests
at their mirrored paths, the s2 pins among them. The `avro` leaf is
listed in the CI table, and the docs, skills, AGENTS.md and the API
inventory name `yggdryl_avro`.
