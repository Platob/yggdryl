Make MarketMessage the one message and FixMsg the codec's handle over it

`MarketMessage` is the market side's own message (D37): a concrete struct in
`graph/message.rs` holding every element, event, market and operation fact
typed in one boxed holder, the facts its source stated, the entries a
protocol stated beyond them as the one row shape under the dictionary's
shared root, its children - a book message's levels, each a message -
its metadata, its anomalies and what it states of its instrument; the four
traits are implemented once on it, so the walk, the merge, the digest, the
writer and the book read a held message with no downcast and no `dyn`.
`MarketData::Message` holds it inline and `MarketKind::Message` names it;
the S3 trait, `Box<dyn MarketMessage>` and `as_message::<T>()` are gone.

The FIX codec converts both ways through its registry: `FixMsg` is the
handle over a message - `into_message` moves it out, `from_message` re-roots
one onto this dictionary - the fixed row interleaves the fact columns and
the entries by a per-registry index so its definition, order, dump and hash
are unmoved, a tag the idmap maps onto a fact is never an entry, and a
message no FIX parse built renders under the standard tags the idmap names
for its facts, else their crate tags, so `parse(render(m))` restates `m`.
`FixAnomaly` is `Anomaly` in `graph/`, `FixLifted` is the record.

The dictionary hash, the census, the crate dump, the equivalence snapshot,
the 912-byte `MarketData` pin and every cost row are unmoved; Python gains
`MarketMessage` and `as_message`; Node's `asFix` is re-spelled `asMessage`.
