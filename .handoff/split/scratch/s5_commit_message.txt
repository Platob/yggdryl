Ship yggdryl-market for Python and npm and teach the release every crate

The market and FIX doors leave the core's two binding packages for a package
of their own on each runtime, `yggdryl-market`, over the one native module the
core already ships (D11 option d): nothing native is built twice, and the
market package is a pure language layer that pins the core exactly.

Python: `python/market/` is the `yggdryl-market` distribution, built by
flit_core and holding `yggdryl/market/` alone - `fix`, `graph`, `side`,
`timeinforce`, `marketdatakind`, `marketdatatype`, `identifier`,
`isin_registry`, `eusipa`, and `enums` for the five market listings - with
`yggdryl==<version>` its one dependency. The core's `yggdryl/__init__.py`
extends its `__path__` with `pkgutil.extend_path`, so `yggdryl.market` is a
portion of the core's namespace wherever either is installed. `State`'s two
FIX readings become `yggdryl.market.fix.state_from_status` and
`state_from_msgtype`. The market suite moves to `python/market/tests/` with its
own `conftest.py` and the capture pipeline it reads; `mypy --strict` declares
both folders as package bases in the one configuration.

Node: `node/market/` is the `yggdryl-market` package, its peer the exact core
version. The core's loader hands the addon's market natives over through the
`yggdryl/implementer` subpath and keeps none of their doors;
`node/scripts/market-natives.js` classifies them from the Rust binding sources
and writes the hand-over list, the market's `index.js` and `index.d.ts`, and
the build and the package audit run it. `book.js` and the market tests and
benchmarks move with the doors; the core suite stops naming the market, the
two FIX-driven logging tests moving to the market's suite.

CI: rows `python-market` and `node-market` run the two suites, the wheel
smoke's market half and the market package audit; every suite that reads the
market installs it beside the core; the `[leaves]` lines of `yggdryl-market`
and `yggdryl-fix` name the two jobs.

Release: `scripts/release_packages.py` is the one list of what a version
publishes - every crate in dependency order, the two PyPI and npm names - and
the version every manifest must agree on. Preflight reads every name on every
registry and refuses a version some carry and others lack; every crate is
dry-run and published in order; the market wheel, sdist and npm package are
built once, smoke-tested on every platform beside the core's and published
after it. `yggdryl-cli` is `publish = false`.

AGENTS.md sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 say the same; `.api-bindings.txt` re-homes
the market entries under their own sections.
