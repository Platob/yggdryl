Move the Parquet medium into yggdryl-parquet

Parquet is the crate `yggdryl-parquet` at `rust/parquet/`: the reader
and writer, the footer cache `ParquetFooter`, the footer statistics
doors, geospatial and metadata, with its tests, the Parquet rows of the
shared record and holder benchmarks, and the PyIceberg and Spark
exchanges as its CI leaf. `install()` claims `PARQUET_CODEC` on the
core's register once per process at rank 1, which
`media::RESERVED_RANKS` keeps for it, so every options value hashes and
orders as it did. The bindings' init calls it, and so does the CLI's
`main` under its `parquet` feature, which `iceberg` implies. The crate's
hidden `implementer` carries the footer, reader, encoder, statistics and
read-ahead bound Iceberg reaches.

The core no longer holds `parquet` or its seed claim. Its `parquet`
feature remains only because `iceberg` implies it for footer metadata.
`claim` admits a reserved rank under its own name alone.
`yggdryl::implementer` gains the projection, landing and extension-name
items the crate reaches beside the shared media section, and `PlanCache`
moves into it. The core tests that build Parquet objects move
byte-identical to the crate's tests at their mirrored paths, the s2 pins
among them. Generated internals nest inside a crate root's own
`internals`. The docs, skills, AGENTS.md and the API inventory name
`yggdryl_parquet`.
